# Research Plan

## Goal

Prepare a photography-focused trip plan for Bosque del Apache, with lodging options and location recommendations organized around sunrise, mid-morning, and sunset.

## Research Areas

### Lodging

To research later:

- Hotels and motels near Socorro, San Antonio, and the refuge area.
- Any locally relevant cabins, guesthouses, inns, or vacation rentals.
- Distance to key refuge access points.
- Early-morning departure practicality.
- Cancellation policy, availability, and current price ranges.

### Photography Locations

To research later:

- Sunrise locations.
- Mid-morning locations.
- Sunset locations.
- Seasonal subject expectations.
- Road access, viewing decks, trails, parking, and closures.
- Lens recommendations and likely compositions.

## Open Questions

- Target travel dates.
- Number of travelers.
- Lodging budget and comfort preferences.
- Whether the priority is birds, landscapes, mammals, abstract light, or a mix.
- Whether mobility, road conditions, or rental-car constraints matter.
- Whether the trip should include nearby photography side trips.

## Research Output Format

When research begins, organize findings into:

- Lodging shortlist.
- Photography-location shortlist.
- Time-of-day shooting plan.
- Practical logistics and current restrictions.
- Sources and date checked.

## Status

Updated June 3, 2026.

Official refuge facts come from U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service pages. Deck, parking, restroom, food, fuel, and lodging pins come from OpenStreetMap/Overpass data and should be treated as planning coordinates, not a substitute for posted refuge maps, booking confirmations, or same-day staff guidance.

## Official Access Notes

- Visitor Center: `33.804777, -106.890917`, 1001 State Highway 1, San Antonio, NM 87832.
- Visitor Center hours: Thursday through Monday, 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
- Friends Nature Store hours: Thursday through Monday, 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
- Refuge lands, tour loop, wilderness, and trails: daily, one hour before sunrise to one hour after sunset, including federal holidays.
- Flush toilets are adjacent to the visitor center and are listed by FWS as open 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
- Vault toilets are at the Flight Deck and Rio Viejo/Bike Trails parking lots and are listed by FWS as open when the tour loop is open.
- Water refill is inside the visitor center and is available concurrently with visitor-center hours; potable water is not available on the Auto Tour Loop.
- Auto Tour Loop: about 12 miles, with a two-way road bisecting the loop.
- Road use: observe one-way directional signs, stay on designated roads and turnouts, and pull fully aside before stopping.
- Posted speeds: maximum 25 mph, with some sections posted 10 mph.
- Daily private vehicle entrance fee: $5.
- No drones, camping, overnight parking, off-road driving, closed-area entry, feeding wildlife, or harassment of wildlife.

## Practical Photography Notes

- Treat Highway 1 and crane-pond pins as legal-parking checks first and photo positions second.
- Do not stand on road shoulders, railroad property, closed areas, or private land for a better angle.
- Flight Deck, Willow Deck, Coyote Deck, Dabbler Deck, and Farm Deck are useful anchors, but bird use changes with water management, crop activity, weather, and disturbance.
- One-way-road notes should be confirmed from posted refuge signage on arrival; the Auto Tour Loop includes one-way sections and a two-way connector.
- Potable water should not be assumed on the loop. Refill at lodging and the visitor center when open.
- For the exact travel week, ask staff where cranes and geese have been roosting and feeding, then reprioritize dawn and sunset locations around that intel.

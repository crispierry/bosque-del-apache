## Sunrise Ranking

1. Flight Deck / Main Pool if water and geese are present.
2. North Crane Pond / Highway 1 crane pools if cranes are roosting there.
3. South Loop first wetland stretch to Dabbler Deck and Eagle Scout Deck.

## Mid-Morning Ranking

1. North Loop / Farm Deck crop-field area after fly-out.
2. Willow Deck and Coyote Deck.
3. Dabbler Deck and Eagle Scout Deck if South Loop wetlands are active.
4. Rio Viejo / Bike Trail restroom area.
5. Visitor Center / Desert Arboretum for close subjects when open or accessible.

## Sunset Ranking

1. North Crane Pond / Highway 1 crane pools if cranes are returning there.
2. Flight Deck / Main Pool if birds are returning there.
3. North Loop / Farm Deck crop-field area if gates are open and birds are staging.

## Road And Parking Notes

- The Auto Tour Loop is about 12 miles and is bisected by a two-way road.
- Official guidance says to observe one-way directional signs, stay on designated roads and turnouts, pull over safely to let others pass, and drive carefully on gravel roads.
- The posted maximum speed is 25 mph, with some sections posted at 10 mph.
- Treat every coordinate as a navigation aid, not permission to park. Use posted pullouts and refuge signage first.
- Current closures, burns, road work, water levels, and active bird concentrations should be checked again in the final travel week and on arrival.

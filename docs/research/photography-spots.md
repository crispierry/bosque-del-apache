# Photography Spots

## Status

Initial research captured. Exact coordinates still need to be added and verified on a map.

## High-Priority Spots

| Spot | Best Window | Exact Practical Position | Subjects | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Old Highway 1 / north approach crane pools | Sunrise, sunset | Safe shoulder or pullouts near shallow pools north of the visitor center | Sandhill cranes, snow/Ross's geese, ducks | Classic crane-pool work. Watch traffic and railroad. Stay off tracks and closed/private areas. |
| Highway 1 west-side crane pools / South Crane Pond | Sunrise, sunset | West-side crane-pool pullouts; pick the legal pullout with active roosting birds | Cranes landing/taking off, geese overhead, reflections | Strong final-light option. Arrive 60-75 minutes before sunset. |
| Flight Deck / Main Pool | Sunrise, sunset | Flight Deck platform/parking, if scouting confirms water and birds | Snow goose blast-off, cranes, ducks, flock patterns | Can be crowded. Main Pool conditions vary with water management. |
| South Loop: Dabbler and Diver Deck / Boardwalk area | Sunrise, mid-morning | First wetland stretch and deck stops on South Loop | Ducks, geese, coots, herons, meadowlarks, Say's phoebe, marsh birds | Good first-day scouting after visitor center intel. Respect one-way road sections. |
| North Loop / Farm Loop crop fields | Mid-morning, sunset | Pullouts around crop fields, north sharp corner, Farm Extension junction if open | Feeding cranes/geese, harriers, bald eagles | Productive after fly-out. Farm Extension may be gated or closed. |
| Willow Deck and Coyote Deck | Mid-morning | Deck viewpoints on North Loop | Ducks, cranes/geese, raptors, passerines | Shared tripod spaces; keep setup compact. |
| Boardwalk / marsh trailhead / Rio Viejo-Bike Trail parking area | Mid-morning | Boardwalk and adjacent open trailhead/parking areas | Marsh birds, ducks, coots, sparrows, quail, roadrunner, deer, coyote | Good lower-pressure session and change of pace. Potable water not on loop. |
| Visitor Center / Desert Arboretum / central two-way road | Mid-morning | Visitor center area, arboretum, and nearby legal pullouts | Roadrunner, Gambel's quail, sparrows, phoebes, Cooper's hawk | Useful close-subject final morning. Vehicle as blind; do not approach wildlife. |
| North Loop fields near Phil Norton Observation Blind | Sunset | Field-view pullouts near blind / Farm Extension junction if open | Cranes/geese staging, roost flights | Confirm same day; gates can close for maintenance or burns. |

## Sunrise Ranking

1. Flight Deck / Main Pool if water and geese are present.
2. Highway 1 crane pools if cranes are roosting there.
3. South Loop first wetland stretch to Dabbler and Diver Deck.

## Mid-Morning Ranking

1. North Loop / Farm Loop crop fields after fly-out.
2. Willow Deck and Coyote Deck.
3. Boardwalk / marsh trailhead / Rio Viejo-Bike Trail area.
4. Visitor Center / Desert Arboretum for close subjects.

## Sunset Ranking

1. Highway 1 west-side crane pools / South Crane Pond.
2. Flight Deck / Main Pool if birds are returning there.
3. North Loop fields near Phil Norton Observation Blind / Farm Extension junction if open.

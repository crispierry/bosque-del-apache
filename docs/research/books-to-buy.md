## Tight Buying Stack

Buy these first:

1. `Secrets To Stunning Bird-In-Flight Photography` by Steve Perry.
2. `Mastering Bird Photography` by Marie Read.
3. `Photographing Birds at Bosque Del Apache` by Scott Bourne.
4. One local-context guide: `The Bird Photographer's Guide To Bosque del Apache` by Tim Boyer if obtainable, or `New Mexico Bird Finding Guide` if broader bird-location context is more useful.

## Read First

### Before Technique Practice

- Steve Perry, `Secrets To Stunning Bird-In-Flight Photography`: autofocus, shutter speed, metering, tracking, panning, takeoffs, and burst discipline.
- Marie Read, `Mastering Bird Photography`: focusing, exposure, seeing the light, composition basics, behavior, on-the-wing shots, and water/mood chapters.

### Before Itinerary Planning

- Scott Bourne, `Photographing Birds at Bosque Del Apache`: where to go, what to bring, and what to expect.
- Tim Boyer, `The Bird Photographer's Guide To Bosque del Apache`: location-specific tactics if a copy can be obtained.

### Before Field Identification

- Stan Tekiela, `Birds of New Mexico Field Guide`, or the `New Mexico Bird Finding Guide`.

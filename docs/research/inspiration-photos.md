# Inspiration Photos

## Status

Initial research captured. Licenses must be rechecked before any website asset is published.

## Use Policy

- Public-domain or clearly permissive-license images may be candidates for the website.
- Photographer portfolio, blog, publication, or social images are inspiration only unless permission/license is explicit.
- Every image candidate needs source, creator, URL, and license notes.

## Themes

- Sunrise fly-outs.
- Sunset fly-ins.
- Sandhill crane behavior.
- Snow goose blast-offs.
- Wetlands and reflections.
- Silhouettes.
- Environmental bird scenes.
- Wider landscape context.

## Visual Direction Takeaways

Use public-domain USFWS/USGS images for real site assets where possible. For the website style, the strongest motifs are low golden horizon, birds as dark shapes over warm skies, mass white-flock texture, mirror-water reflections, and wider environmental scenes that show ponds, fields, decks, and mountains.

For non-government and portfolio work, use the images only as composition and timing inspiration unless permission is obtained.

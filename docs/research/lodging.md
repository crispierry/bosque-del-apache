# Lodging Research

## Status

Initial lodging research captured June 2, 2026. Prices are date-sensitive and should be rechecked before booking.

## Strategy

For early December sunrise work, optimize around refuge access hours:

- Refuge lands and the tour loop open one hour before sunrise to one hour after sunset.
- Early December sunrise near Socorro is roughly around 7:00 AM.
- Socorro departures around 5:30-5:45 AM are sensible for setup time.
- San Antonio options are closer, but have smaller inventory and more B&B-style constraints.

## Special-Case Option

### Fite Ranch Bed & Breakfast

- Area: near San Antonio.
- Drive: property says short 15-minute drive to Bosque del Apache; budget 15-25 minutes depending target access point.
- Why it fits: quiet ranch stay, full kitchens, close to refuge, potentially good for midday breaks.
- Watchouts: remote, limited inventory, manual booking, rates and cancellation terms not posted.
- Sources: [Fite Ranch](https://fiteranch.com/), [Stay page](https://fiteranch.com/stay-with-us/), [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g47191-d782969-Reviews-Fite_Ranch_Bed_Breakfast-San_Antonio_New_Mexico.html)

## Nearby Backup

### Truth Or Consequences

Use only if Socorro/San Antonio are full or if hot springs become part of the trip.

- Drive: roughly 60-70 minutes before dawn to the refuge via I-25 exit 115 and NM-1.
- Practical chain choice: Holiday Inn Express Truth or Consequences, around $115+ on KAYAK.
- Nicer but less budget-aligned: Sierra Grande Lodge, around $191+.

Sources: [FWS south approach](https://www.fws.gov/refuge/bosque-del-apache/visit-us), [HIX T or C Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/us/truth-or-consequences.html), [KAYAK](https://www.kayak.com/Truth-or-Consequences-Hotels-Holiday-Inn-Express-Suites-Truth-OR-Consequences.448384.ksp), [Sierra Grande Expedia](https://www.expedia.com/Truth-Or-Consequences-Hotels-Sierra-Grande-Lodge.h17836823.Hotel-Information)

## Avoid / Watch Out

- Avoid Albuquerque or Las Cruces as daily sunrise bases. The drive is too long for repeated pre-dawn departures.
- Be cautious with San Miguel Inn, Super 8 Socorro, and Baymont Socorro unless budget pressure is severe; current/recent review summaries show more cleanliness and maintenance variability.

Sources: [San Miguel Inn Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g47216-d125802-Reviews-San_Miguel_Inn-Socorro_New_Mexico.html), [Super 8 Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/us/super8.html), [Baymont Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g47216-d543015-Reviews-Baymont_by_Wyndham_Socorro-Socorro_New_Mexico.html)

## Bottom Line

- Book Holiday Inn Express Socorro for the cleanest low-friction trip.
- Book Casa Blanca if the closest birding-oriented stay matters most and B&B policies are acceptable.
- Book Comfort Inn if Holiday Inn pricing jumps.

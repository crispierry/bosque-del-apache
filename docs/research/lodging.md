## Strategy

For early December sunrise work, optimize around refuge access hours:

- Refuge lands and the tour loop open one hour before sunrise to one hour after sunset.
- Candidate-window sunrise near Socorro ranges from 6:53-6:58 AM for Option A and 6:59-7:03 AM for Option B.
- Socorro departures around 5:30-5:45 AM are sensible for setup time, especially for crane-pond or Flight Deck mornings.
- San Antonio options are closer, but have smaller inventory and more B&B-style constraints.
- Book refundable/direct when possible until weather, bird reports, final dates, and camera travel logistics are locked.
- There does not appear to be a Marriott Bonvoy property in Socorro or San Antonio. The practical Bonvoy candidate is in Los Lunas, which is a loyalty/status option rather than a photography-first dawn base.

## Marriott Bonvoy Option

### Fairfield by Marriott Inn & Suites Los Lunas

- Area: Los Lunas, New Mexico.
- Address: 2255 Sun Ranch Village Loop SW, Los Lunas, NM 87031.
- Drive reality: roughly 75-80 miles / about 1 hour 20 minutes to the Bosque del Apache Visitor Center by route estimate. Recheck with the final dawn target, road conditions, and weather before booking.
- Sunrise impact: for the December 6-12 preferred window, a one-hour-before-sunrise setup means arriving around 5:59-6:03 AM. From Los Lunas, that implies leaving around 4:15-4:30 AM. From Socorro, the same setup is more like a 5:25-5:35 AM departure.
- Why consider it: Marriott Bonvoy earning/redemption, newer Fairfield brand fit, free breakfast, free parking, fitness center, and a predictable chain-hotel experience.
- Watchouts: not close to Bosque del Apache, exact direct-booking rate still needs confirmation, likely limited review history, and breakfast may not help on sunrise mornings.
- Recommendation: consider it only if Bonvoy points/status strongly matter, if Socorro lodging is unexpectedly poor or overpriced, or as a first/last Albuquerque-area positioning night. For a photography-first five-day plan, Socorro is still the better base.
- Sources: [Marriott official page](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/abqlf-fairfield-inn-and-suites-los-lunas/overview/), [Marriott New Mexico hotels](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/destinations/united-states/new-mexico.mi)

## Bottom Line

- Prefer December 6-12 if the goal is lower crowd pressure and potentially easier lodging.
- Prefer November 29-December 5 if Festival of the Cranes programming matters.
- Book Comfort Inn & Suites Socorro first if the direct/refundable Dec 6-12 total stays close to the Expedia snapshot.
- Book Best Western Socorro if Comfort Inn cancellation terms are poor or Best Western direct/refundable pricing is better.
- Book Holiday Inn Express Socorro only if the cleanest low-friction chain stay is worth the higher exact-date total.
- Book Casa Blanca only if the closest birding-oriented stay matters most and B&B policies are acceptable.
- Consider Fairfield by Marriott Inn & Suites Los Lunas only as a Marriott Bonvoy tradeoff, not the default sunrise base.
- Do not book nonrefundable lodging until final dates, room needs, cancellation terms, and recent reviews have been checked for the exact stay.

## Booking-Time Verification Checklist

- Exact nightly total after taxes and fees.
- Free-cancellation deadline in local hotel time.
- Breakfast hours and whether a grab-and-go option is available before a 5:30-5:45 AM departure.
- Parking ease for a photo gear-heavy vehicle.
- Recent reviews from the last 3-6 months, especially cleanliness, heat, hot water, noise, bed comfort, and safety.
- Pet, accessibility, and room-location needs if relevant.
- Distance to food/fuel that is open after sunset and before dawn.
